# The Garden Collection

Approved creative direction: 26 October 2026. Fictional demonstration concept for Ewig & Eie, not real clients or a completed commission.

## Couple bible

Clara September, 30 at concept approval, is a Cape Town landscape architect: observant, quietly funny, and fond of hosting. Her notebook mixes planting combinations with overheard conversations. She forgets to put the bread on the table.

Ruben de Villiers, 32 at concept approval, is a documentary sound editor: patient, sociable, particular about sound and lemon in a salad. He remembers coffee orders and labels leftovers.

They live together in a rented Cape Town flat and speak English and Afrikaans. They cook, see family, and attempt repairs that become larger projects. Ages need not appear in guest-facing copy.

March 2022: a mutual friend's courtyard supper. Clara brings a folding table; Ruben brings a repaired chair for the host. They talk while washing dishes about the host's struggling lemon tree. First date: a nursery and lunch; he buys a pot, she later sends placement advice.

2022: Sunday lunches become a habit, sometimes just toast. 2024: they move into their flat; their first joint purchase is a balcony table that barely fits through the door. 2025: family recipe project, with Clara transcribing and Ruben recording relatives' explanations. June 2026: after recording his grandmother's baked pudding recipe, Ruben proposes at their balcony table with a handwritten letter. Clara reads it twice before saying yes.

## Art direction

Botanical, romantic, understated, contemporary. Let natural skin, linen, paper and stone carry the warmth. Avoid heavy sepia, cluttered floral frames, scripted body copy, or wedding-industry superlatives. Photographs are styled fictional wedding concept imagery illustrating a future celebration, not documentation of a past wedding.

| Token | Hex | Role |

Use olive for text on the light palette; do not use sage or rose as small text on ivory. Do not rely on colour alone for RSVP status. Keep texture behind reading areas extremely subtle.

Typography suggestion: Cormorant Garamond 500–600 for names and headings; Source Sans 3 400–600 for body and controls. Serif fallback Georgia; sans fallback system-ui. No fonts are downloaded or installed in this pack. Suggested mobile body 17–18px, line-height 1.5–1.6; heading 36–48px; labels at least 14px. Keep paragraphs short, sentence case, with generous spacing. Main chat can map these to its existing typography system.
