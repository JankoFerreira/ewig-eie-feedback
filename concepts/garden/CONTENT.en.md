## Our story
### It started with the washing-up.
At a friend's courtyard supper in March 2022, Clara brought a folding table and Ruben brought a chair he'd repaired for the host. We met properly at the kitchen sink, discussing a lemon tree neither of us owned.

Our first date was a nursery visit and lunch. Ruben bought one pot. Clara bought nothing, then sent very precise instructions about where his pot should go.

Sunday lunches became a habit. In 2024, we moved into a Cape Town flat with a balcony just big enough for a table. In 2025, we began collecting family recipes: Clara wrote them down; Ruben recorded the explanations, including every disagreement about quantities.

In June 2026, after recording his grandmother's baked pudding recipe, Ruben asked Clara to sit outside. There was a letter, a ring, and a very long pause while she read the letter again. Then, yes.

Now we're making room for everyone who helped us get here.

## The venue
### Die Varinghuis
Elgin Valley, Western Cape · Fictional venue

A garden, an old glasshouse, and apple orchards beyond the lawn. We'll exchange vows outside and gather in the glasshouse for supper and dancing.

Parking is beside the entrance. The garden paths include gravel and grass; please tell us about any access needs when you RSVP so we can plan with you. If it rains, the ceremony moves into the glasshouse.

Demo note: Die Varinghuis is invented. No street address, map pin, booking link, or real travel directions are provided.

## What to wear
### Garden formal
Dresses, jumpsuits, or a suit—something you feel lovely in. Choose shoes that are happy on grass and bring a light layer for the evening. The wedding party will wear ivory; guests are welcome to choose any other colour.

## Travel & your stay
Make a weekend of it if you can. For this imagined celebration, we'd suggest staying in the Elgin or Grabouw area and arranging your return transport before the day.

Flying in? Cape Town International is the arrival airport for this sample itinerary. Accommodation and transfers are not included. No room blocks or shuttle service have been arranged in this demo.

When this template is used for a real wedding, the couple will add their confirmed address, recommended stays, and transport details here. Please don't make bookings based on this demonstration.
